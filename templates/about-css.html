Sports

One of my biggest hobbies is playing sports. Without a doubt my favorite sport is Soccer. I've been playing it for more than half of my life and enjoy different aspects of the game such as dribbling and passing. My favorite team is Liverpool. I also like to play ping pong. My dad and I play it a lot, and we are very competitive. Before ping pong we played a lot of pool with each other. We actually got pretty good at the game and were able to make shots at really difficult angles. After he got introduced to ping pong he convinced me to start playing with him, and it eventually replaced pool for us.

Favorite Food Places

When it comes to this topic I mean to talk about food spots that I go to most frequently, which aren't the highest quality but are always satisfactory for me. The top two for me are Chick-fil-A and Chipotle. I definitely eat from either of these spots at least once a week. I usually get the spicy chicken sandwich from Chick-fil-A and customizable bowls from Chipotle. One thing I want to do is explore the menus of the two establishments more. The thing is once I find something I really like, it's natural for me to not want to switch just keep ordering it. I actually recently tried Chick-fil-A's new Chicken & Waffles Sandwich which was nice.

My Favorite Vacation Spots

These locations are my favorite to visit within the United States. Within the United States, my go-to spots are Virginia Beach and Ocean City. I've been to each of these spots at least five times and love the boardwalk, beaches offered. I've also had such a wide variety of foods in both locations, from sushi to fajitas to all kinds of nutella-infused breakfast treats! Definitely not the healthiest at times but it's worth it eat at these locations every once in a while. There are also all kinds of other activities available to visitors, such as amusement parks and arcades that are really fun. For amusement parks specifically, I try to go on new rides whenever I visit these locations!
